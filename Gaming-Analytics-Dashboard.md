# Gaming Analytics & Player Behavior Dashboard

## Business Problem
The objective of this project is to analyse player engagement, churn risk, and revenue performance in a gaming environment. The goal is to identify key factors affecting player retention and optimise campaign performance.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Python
- Excel

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue
- ARPU (Average Revenue per User)
- Churn Risk
- Campaign Spend
- Users at Risk

## Analysis
- Forecasted revenue trends
- Analysed churn distribution
- Compared churn across regions and age groups
- Evaluated campaign performance (CPA vs churn)
- Identified top-performing games
- Analysed platform-wise user distribution

## Key Insights
- Small segment of users contributes most revenue
- High churn observed in specific segments
- Campaign optimisation opportunities identified
- User behaviour patterns impact retention

## Outcome
- Improved understanding of user behaviour
- Supported data-driven decision making
- Identified areas for revenue and retention improvement
